# VibeLive Vision Spec

For Shelley to read and approve.
Written November 5, 2026, from the full VibeLive chat.
Nothing in this spec is built until you say it is exactly right.

## 1. What VibeLive is

VibeLive is your own live streaming social app.
It is for people worldwide. You are in Canada.
It is a separate product from OpenCircle. Never mix them.
It must compete with Kik, MeetMe, and GoLive Streamer.
It must feel like Insta and Facebook Live, but better.

The goal is a real app real people can test and use.
Not a demo. Not a preview. Not a picture of an app.

## 2. The bar

VibeLive must be better and more accommodating than GoLive Streamer and the rest.
That means three things, and every change is judged against them.

Simpler. Fewer steps to sign up, go live, join a stream, and send a gift.
More welcoming. Big readable text, big tap targets, plain words.
Honest empty states and error states. No dead ends.
Generous free tier. No paywalls blocking the core experience.

## 3. Features

Real accounts and profiles.
Sign up, sign in, sign out, stay signed in, password recovery.
Email and password sign in. Not Google only.
A unique username and profile for every user.
Profile picture, cover image, bio, interests, profile links.
One optional Throne link.
Online or offline status, with privacy controls.
Followers, following, friends, blocking, reporting.
A real profile page you can open from streams, chat, viewer lists, and communities.

Real livestreaming.
Start, join, leave, and end a live stream.
Real video and audio across separate devices and accounts.
Camera and microphone permissions, explained in plain words.
Front and rear camera switch. Camera and mic mute.
Connection status, reconnection, loading, and error handling.
Stream titles, descriptions, and categories.
Real viewer counts, from real connected viewers.
Host and moderator permissions.
Stream chat, reactions, and gifts.
Clean ending when a user leaves, disconnects, or ends a stream.
Leave and re-enter a stream without getting stuck or losing the camera.
Camera recovery and reconnection fixed.

Quick Exit.
One tap that at once pauses or hides your outgoing camera and microphone.
Auto pause when the app goes to the background, where the phone allows it.
Privacy status always obvious on screen.

Clickable viewer lists.
The viewer count icon must be clickable.
Tap it and see the real people watching.
Tap a viewer to open their profile.
Follow them, add them as a friend, or start a private chat.
Invite viewers to take part where that is supported.
Moderation controls only for people allowed to use them.
Never fake names. Never invented viewer counts.

Real private messaging.
Conversations saved securely.
Delivery status and read status.
Conversation lists and unread counts.
Text messages and media attachments.
Blocking and reporting inside messages.
Notifications where the phone supports them.
Tapping Message opens or creates the right conversation with the right person.

Feed, reels, and communities.
A real feed from real user posts.
Posts, images, and videos. Likes and comments.
Reels and short videos with real playback.
Creator discovery, search, and categories.
Communities and interest groups.
Events and stream announcements.

Crews.

Coins and credits.

Clear prices, clear history, clear confirmation.

No spending more than you have. No self gifting.

Reels and video clips.

Retention, without annoying people.

Community activities and special events.

Design and usability.

Readable text. Comfortable font sizes. Strong contrast.
